## Ohue Laboratory

The laboratory applies information science to life science, chemistry, and drug discovery to accelerate innovation.

{{< contact >}}

## Laboratory visits

Visits for undergraduate research placement in the Department of Computer Science are usually held in February and March. Please contact Ohue by email in advance. Visits at other times may also be possible. Confirm the meeting room when arranging your visit.

## Students

The laboratory accepts fourth-year undergraduate research students in the Department of Computer Science and master's and doctoral students in the Graduate Majors in Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence, School of Computing, Institute of Science Tokyo.

Applicants to the master's or doctoral program should pass the relevant graduate entrance examination with Ohue as their prospective supervisor. Refer to the [official admissions website](https://admissions.isct.ac.jp/en/) for current procedures and dates.

### Research supervision for students enrolled elsewhere

Students enrolled in another school or institution may conduct research in the laboratory through an agreed research-supervision arrangement. This requires agreement between both laboratories. Please inquire by email first.

## Researchers and staff

### JSPS Postdoctoral Fellows (PD and RPD)

The laboratory hosts JSPS PD and RPD fellows in bioinformatics, drug discovery informatics, and related areas. Please contact Ohue before applying. Discussion and support can begin at the research-planning and application-writing stages.

### Postdoctoral researchers, technical staff, and administrative staff

Open positions are announced on the website. Opportunities to host researchers may also be available when no position is advertised. Please contact Ohue to discuss joining the laboratory.

## Non-degree research students

Except in special circumstances, the laboratory does not accept non-degree research students before they have passed an official entrance examination. Prospective international students are encouraged to consider the embassy-recommended MEXT Scholarship or the International Graduate Program (IGP(C)). The laboratory welcomes successful applicants through the university's official admission processes.

See the [official admissions website](https://admissions.isct.ac.jp/en/) for current requirements.
