## Education and career

- April 2002: Entered the Department of Electronics and Information Engineering, Ishikawa National College of Technology.
- March 2007: Graduated at the top of the class from Ishikawa National College of Technology.
- April 2007: Transferred into the third year of the Department of Computer Science, Faculty of Engineering, Tokyo Institute of Technology.
- March 2009: B.Eng., Tokyo Institute of Technology.
- April 2009: Entered the master's program in the Department of Computer Science, Graduate School of Information Science and Engineering, Tokyo Institute of Technology.
- March 2011: M.Eng., Tokyo Institute of Technology.
- March 2014: Ph.D. in Engineering, Tokyo Institute of Technology (degree number 6564).
- April 2011 to March 2014: JSPS Research Fellow (DC1).
- April 2014 to March 2015: JSPS Research Fellow (PD) and Research Fellow, Tokyo Institute of Technology.
- April 2015 to March 2016: Assistant Professor, Department of Computer Science, Graduate School of Information Science and Engineering, Tokyo Institute of Technology.
- April 2016 to March 2020: Assistant Professor, Department of Computer Science, School of Computing, Tokyo Institute of Technology (following an organizational restructuring).
- April 2016 to March 2020: Concurrent appointment in the Smart Drug Discovery Research Unit, Institute of Innovative Research, Tokyo Institute of Technology.
- April 2020 to December 2023: Tenure-track Assistant Professor, Department of Computer Science, School of Computing, Tokyo Institute of Technology.
- January 2024 to present: Associate Professor, Department of Computer Science, School of Computing. The institution became Institute of Science Tokyo in October 2024.
